[+me]{.long} is a Senior Research Scientist +EOSL at +GTRI.  +me earned
his Ph.D.  in Physics from the Georgia Institute of Technology School of
Physics in 2016. +me's current work focuses on simulating realistic
imagery across the electro-optical and infrared spectrum.  +me is a lead
developer of +GTSIMS in which he works to improve the representation of
light propagation through the atmosphere, incorporation of background
clutter features, and generally improve the interoperability and
efficiency of the +GTSIMS of the GTSIMS framework.  +me is also the
maintainer of +GTSIG and its first principle thermal modeling
capabilities which is an integral part of infrared signature prediction.
+me is the course administrator for the Infrared/Visible Signature
Suppression course offered by +EOSL as part of the Certificate in
Infrared and Electro-Optical Technology through Georgia Tech
Professional Education.  This course covers: basic signature concerns
for ground, sea, and air vehicles, modeling and analysis techniques with
a focus on vehicle signatures, and an overview of suppression
techniques.  +me's research interests include: near field radiative
transfer between closely nanoscale objects, radiative transfer through
participating media in complex geometries, electromagnetic wave
scattering from arbitrary geometries, and solving sparse, symmetric,
systems of non-linear equations.
